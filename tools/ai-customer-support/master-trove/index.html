What's inside your Master Trove

  • Your ticket-to-reply flow written out step by step, from the query landing to the reply going out
  • The approval layer specified - what a second AI model checks before anything is sent, and what it does with each verdict
  • The escalation path - what happens when a reply fails the check twice, who gets tagged and where
  • The history lookup written for your situation - using your CRM if you have one, and what to put in place first if you don't
  • What each piece of the setup is for, so you can tell which parts you already have
  • Your build options - do it yourself, hand it to a developer, or give it to your team - with a brief you can paste into a hiring post
  • What to have ready before anyone starts: your product journey, your forms, your billing and escalation rules, your refunds and offers
  • The whole blueprint as a file you load into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, so you can keep asking it questions
  • A setup guide PDF that takes you from the download to your first question

This is a blueprint, not software. Nothing you receive connects to your helpdesk, reads a ticket, drafts a reply or posts to Slack. It is the plan - so you, your team or a developer can build it.
