During the call

It does the work, not just the talking

While the client is on the line, AI Caller works with your real calendar and records what happened.

  • Checks live slotsOffers only times that are free on your calendar right now.
  • Books or reschedulesPuts the session on the calendar, or moves an existing one.
  • Checks a bookingIf a client is unsure when their session is, it looks it up.
  • Schedules a callbackIf now is a bad time, it agrees a better one and calls back then.
  • Logs the outcomeEvery call result is written to your CRM, so nothing lives in someone's head.
  • Tells your teamYour team gets a message the moment a call is booked.

Guardrails

Polite by design, not by luck

A phone call reaches a real person. These rules are built into the software and checked before every call.

  • Sensible hours onlyCalls only between 9 am and 8 pm in the client's own time zone.
  • Limits on retriesA daily cap on calls to each person, and it stops after 10 rounds of trying.
  • No pointless callsSkips anyone who has already booked or has left your program.
  • "Do not call" is finalIf someone asks not to be called, further calls to them are blocked.
  • Honest about being AIWhen asked, it says it is an AI assistant calling for your business.
  • Verified caller IDCalls come from a verified number, not an unknown one.

Questions

What owners ask first

Is it a robot voice?

It holds a real two-way conversation: it listens, answers and books in the same call. It does not pretend to be human. When someone asks, it says it is an AI assistant calling for your business.

What is a playbook?

A playbook is a pre-built call for one moment in the customer journey, such as a new lead or a no-show. Its goal, timing and rules are already set. You choose which playbooks to switch on, and we configure them with you.

What does it connect to?

Your calendar, to see free slots and make bookings, and your CRM, to know who to call and to log every outcome. It can also message your team when a call is booked. We confirm your exact setup in the demo.

Which languages does it speak?

English today.

What does it cost?

Pilot pricing is agreed with each business. We cover it in the demo.

Do I have to change my systems?

Usually not. AI Caller connects to your calendar and CRM. Today it works with Zoho CRM and OnceHub. Other systems are confirmed in the demo, so tell us what you use. Your team keeps working the way it does now, with the follow-up calls taken off their list.

Is it ready to use today?

Not as self-serve. It ran in production at Coach Foundation, our sister company, from March 2026, and is currently paused while we prepare the pilot. We are opening it to a small number of businesses, set up together with you.
